# Campaign Chronicles

> **Every campaign leaves a story.**

**Campaign Chronicles** is a web application designed to preserve, organize, and connect the story of a tabletop role-playing campaign.

Campaigns grow quickly.

Sessions become memories. Characters evolve. NPCs return months later. Locations gain meaning. Quests change direction. Important objects appear, disappear, and sometimes become part of the history of the entire group.

Campaign Chronicles brings all of that information together in one place.

---

## The Memory of Your Campaign

Campaign Chronicles is designed around a simple idea:

> **A campaign deserves more than scattered notes.**

Instead of keeping campaign information across documents, chats, notebooks, character sheets, and disconnected tools, Campaign Chronicles creates a persistent archive of the adventure.

It is designed for **tabletop RPGs in general** and is not tied to a specific game system or genre.

Fantasy, science fiction, cyberpunk, horror, mystery, modern settings and homebrew worlds can all live inside the same platform.

---

## Features

### Campaigns

Create and manage independent campaigns, each with its own identity, information, artwork, and history.

### Session Journal

Preserve what happened during each session and build a chronological record of the adventure.

### Characters

Keep track of the player characters who shape the story.

### NPC Archive

Remember allies, enemies, mysterious strangers, recurring characters, and everyone the party meets along the way.

### Locations

Build an archive of important places discovered throughout the campaign.

Cities, kingdoms, dungeons, planets, taverns, ruins, hideouts, landmarks and anything else worth remembering can become part of the campaign record.

### Quest Tracker

Follow the objectives and storylines that drive the adventure.

### Items & Loot

Keep track of important equipment, artifacts, treasures, clues, rewards and memorable objects discovered during the campaign.

### Campaign Notes

Preserve information that doesn't belong anywhere else but still matters to the story.

### Global Search

Quickly find information across the campaign without digging through months of notes.

### Import & Export

Campaign Chronicles provides tools for bringing existing campaign information into the platform and exporting campaign records into readable documents.

### Profiles

Players can create their own identity within Campaign Chronicles while keeping their individual campaign characters separate.

### English & Spanish

The interface supports both English and Spanish.

Campaign content remains exactly as written by its users.

### Responsive Experience

Campaign Chronicles is designed for desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile use.

Your campaign archive can stay with you whether you're preparing at your computer or checking something during a session.

---

## Not Another Virtual Tabletop

Campaign Chronicles is **not a Virtual Tabletop**.

It doesn't try to replace the tools used for maps, combat, dice rolling, character automation or game rules.

Those tools help you **play** the campaign.

Campaign Chronicles helps you **remember it**.

> **Campaign Chronicles is the memory of the campaign.**

---

## Built for Every Kind of Adventure

Campaign Chronicles is intentionally system-agnostic.

Its design and structure are meant to work across different tabletop RPG genres without forcing the campaign into the visual identity or rules of one particular game.

The adventure defines the world.

Campaign Chronicles preserves it.

## The Idea Behind Campaign Chronicles

Campaigns eventually end.

Characters retire.

Groups change.

Years pass.

But somehow people still remember that impossible roll, the NPC nobody trusted, the city they accidentally destroyed, the artifact they should never have touched, or the joke that somehow became canon.

Those moments become part of the group's history.

**Campaign Chronicles was created to give that history a home.**
